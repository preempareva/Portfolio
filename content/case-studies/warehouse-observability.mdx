## The situation

At Media.net many teams share the same data warehouse for reporting and analysis.

## The problem

There was no structured view of who used the warehouse, where the time went, or when queries queued up.

## Why it mattered

Without that view, slow periods and heavy workloads were hard to explain or plan around.

## Constraints

- Usage data had to stay internal; this write-up stays at a high level on purpose.
- The dashboard needed to work for people who don't read raw system tables.

## Investigation and discovery

The built-in query history only covered a short default window, too short to show trends over time.

## Approach

Move the reporting onto a persisted dataset so history builds up, then put a dashboard on top that starts with the daily trend.

## Implementation

A query-performance dashboard that shows warehouse usage and query behaviour over time and lets people drill from a daily trend down to the queries behind it.

## Tools and systems

SQL, Redshift, Tableau.

## Outcome

Teams can drill from a daily trend straight to the queries behind it, which wasn't possible before.

## What I learned

Check how much history a source actually keeps before building on it.

## What I would improve next

Add alerting on unusual queueing, so the dashboard flags problems instead of waiting to be opened.
